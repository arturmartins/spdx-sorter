# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background or identity. We expect respectful, constructive
collaboration in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

Use inclusive language, assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback,
and respect differing viewpoints and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing,
unwanted sexual attention, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may remove
content, issue warnings, or restrict participation. Reports are handled as
confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter is not tolerated.

This compact policy is adapted from the principles of the Contributor Covenant
and applies to all project spaces.
